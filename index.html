Game Outside

Get out. Explore. Play.

Coming soon to the App Store

A card game for exploring the outdoors with your kids. Draw a challenge, go find it, decide together whether it counts. Then draw the next one.

How it works

  1. Pick where you are A featured park with challenges written for that exact place, or Play Anywhere — a sidewalk, a back garden, a car park will do.
  2. Draw a card Nobody knows what's on it until you flip it. One challenge at a time, so everyone is looking for the same thing.
  3. Go find it A grown-up decides if it counts, and sneaky answers count too. Stuck? You get a couple of skips.
  4. Finish the deck Seven cards for a quick adventure, up to fifteen for a long one. Finish it and you're Legendary Explorers.

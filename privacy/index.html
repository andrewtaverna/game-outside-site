Privacy Policy

Game Outside · Effective 29 August 2026

The short version: Game Outside does not collect your personal information. There is no account to create, no sign-in, and no analytics. Your photos and your game history are stored on your device and are never uploaded to us.

Who we are

Game Outside is a family game published by Inside the Rink. You can reach us at hello@insidetherink.com.

Information we collect

None. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, or any other personal detail. The app has no accounts and no sign-in of any kind, so there is nothing for us to hold.

What stays on your device

The app saves a few things locally so the game works and remembers where you were. All of it lives on your phone, in the app's own storage, and none of it is transmitted to us:

  • Any photos you choose to take during a game.
  • Your finished and in-progress adventures.
  • Your settings, such as the light or dark appearance.
  • Notes recorded when you mark a challenge as not working at a location.

Deleting the app removes all of it. We cannot recover it, because we never had it.

Photos and the camera

If you take a photo during a game, it is saved on your device and shown back to you in that adventure. Photos are never uploaded, never seen by us, and never shared with anyone. The app asks for camera and photo library access only when you choose to add a photo, and the game is fully playable if you decline.

Network connections

The app connects to the internet for one purpose: to download new and updated challenge cards and locations, and app updates. This is a one-way read — the app asks for content and receives it. Nothing about you, your device, or your game is sent along with the request.

As with any internet request, our content provider (Supabase) and our update provider (Expo) receive the network information inherent to delivering a response, such as an IP address, and may retain it briefly in operational logs. We do not use this to identify or track anyone, and we do not combine it with anything else.

Once content has been downloaded, the game works offline.

Children's privacy

Game Outside is designed to be played by families, with a parent or guardian running the game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the app collects nothing at all, there is no personal information about a child for us to receive, store, use, or disclose.

No advertising, no tracking, no selling

There are no advertisements in Game Outside. There are no third-party advertising networks, no analytics services, and no tracking technologies. We do not sell, rent, or share personal information, because we do not have any.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, or delete personal information a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing to request. If you would like the data on your own device removed, delete the app.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what it collects, we will update this page and change the effective date above before that version is released. If a change were ever material, we would say so in the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy are welcome at hello@insidetherink.com.
